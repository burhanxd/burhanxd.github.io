## Methodology

My approach to bug bounty hunting centers on deep manual testing rather than automated scanning:

- **Recon** - Thorough subdomain enumeration, technology fingerprinting, and attack surface mapping
- **Logic-first** - Focus on business logic flaws, access control issues, and authentication bypasses before looking for injection points
- **API deep-dive** - Reverse engineering API flows, testing authorization at every endpoint, and looking for data exposure
- **Chaining** - Combining low-severity findings into high-impact exploit chains
